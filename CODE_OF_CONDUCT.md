# Code of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our standard

AI Tell Scan is for evidence-led discussion of interfaces and source code. Be
respectful, direct, and constructive. Critique behavior, evidence, tradeoffs,
and implementation rather than a person's ability, intent, identity, or choice
of tools. Respect privacy and licensing boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private repository or report are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community
events, and private project communication. It also applies when someone
publicly represents the project or acts with maintainer authority.

## Reporting and confidentiality

Do not post incident details publicly. Email `security@first-tree.ai` with the
subject `[AI Tell Scan conduct]`. If email is unavailable, open a minimal issue
requesting private maintainer follow-up and include no names, screenshots, or
incident details.

Designated maintainers will limit a report to the people needed to assess and
resolve it, preserve confidentiality where legally and operationally possible,
and avoid assigning the report to a person named in it. They may request more
information, remove content, lock a thread, warn or temporarily restrict a
participant, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces. Deliberately
false reports may receive the same response; good-faith reports will not be
penalized because evidence is incomplete.

Maintainers will explain an enforcement decision privately when possible.
Requests for reconsideration may use the same reporting channel and should name
the decision and any new context.
